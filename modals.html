SentenceWhat it means
Every evening, Appu would play football with his friends. PAST HABITHe did this regularly, again and again in the past.
When Appu was ten, he could run five kilometres without stopping. PAST ABILITYHe had the physical power to do this when he was younger.
Appu should drink plenty of water after playing in the sun. DUTY / ADVICEThis is the right and healthy thing for him to do.
💡 Grammar Secret: Look at the action words right after should, would, could — they are play, run, drink. Do they have -ing, -ed or -s added?   No! They always stay in their simple BASE FORM.

S
The SHOULD Detective — Duty & Good Advice
When should we use "should"? Read the examples and find out!
Pattern Examples
Example 1
Spending time outdoors and enjoying real-life moments should be just as important as the digital world. → This is good advice — it tells us what is right and important.
Example 2
Irma's mother thought Irma should not let herself be carried away by the smartphone. → Her mother is saying what Irma's duty is — the right thing to do.
Example 3
We should respect our elders and teachers. → This is our duty — something we are supposed to do because it is right.
Example 4
You should wear a helmet while riding a bicycle. → Safety advice — the right and sensible thing to do.
Fill in the Blanks
  • 🦷
    Children brush their teeth every night before bed. (It is the right, healthy habit to do.)
    ✅ should — this is good advice / duty. It is the right thing to do.
  • 🌿
    We plant more trees to protect our environment. (This is the right thing to do for the earth.)
    ✅ should — planting trees is our duty to the environment.
  • 📵
    Students limit their screen time and play outdoors more often. (A doctor or teacher is giving this advice.)
    ✅ should — this is good advice about a healthy habit.
  • 1
    In "You should eat vegetables every day" — is the speaker talking about a past memory, OR giving good advice about what is right?
  • 2
    Complete the sentence with should: "We ___ always keep our classroom clean because it is our ___."
WHAT IT DOES: should shows DUTY, OBLIGATION, or GOOD ADVICE — the right or important thing to do.
CLUE WORDS: "important", "right thing", "must", "good habit", "healthy"
GRAMMAR: should is always followed by a BASE VERB — should be, should go, should drink (NOT should being / should went).
W
The WOULD Time-Machine — Past Habits
Something done again and again in the past — a regular habit
Pattern Examples
Example 1
In the evening she would go cycling. → This was her regular habit every evening — not just once.
Example 2
Early in the morning she would have great fun drawing and painting. → She did this repeatedly every morning in the past.
Example 3
She would scroll through social media, watch videos, and play games for hours. → This was her repeated behaviour — a new bad habit she developed.
Example 4
Every Sunday morning, my father would take us to the beach. → Every Sunday, again and again — a regular family habit in the past.
Fill in the Blanks
  • 📖
    When I was in Class 4, my grandmother tell me a bedtime story every night. (She did this every night, regularly.)
    ✅ would — telling a story every night was a repeated past habit.
  • ⚽
    When my grandfather was a boy, he walk five kilometres to school every day. (He did this every day, as a regular thing.)
    ✅ would — walking to school every day was a repeated past habit.
  • 🎨
    Every afternoon, the children gather in the park to play. (They did this every afternoon — a regular habit.)
    ✅ would — gathering every afternoon was a repeated past habit.
  • 1
    Did Irma go cycling only once, or was it her regular habit in the evenings? What word tells you this?
  • 2
    Write a sentence using would about something your family did every Sunday when you were younger.
WHAT IT DOES: would shows PAST HABITS — actions done again and again as a regular routine in the past.
CLUE WORDS: "every day", "every evening", "in the morning", "when I was young", "regularly"
GRAMMAR: would is always followed by a BASE VERB — would go, would have, would tell (NOT would went / would going).
C
The COULD Power Meter — Past Ability
What someone was able (or not able) to do in the past
Pattern Examples
Example 1
She could ride for a long time without getting tired. → She had the stamina and ability to ride — a physical skill she had in the past.
Example 2
She found she could not take her eyes off the screen. → She was unable to stop looking — she lacked the ability to resist. (could not = negative ability)
Example 3
Often she could not help reaching for her phone even when she was busy. → She was not able to stop herself — she had lost the ability to resist.
Example 4
Grandpa could read small print without glasses when he was young. → He had sharp eyesight (an ability) when he was younger.
Fill in the Blanks
  • 🎹
    At the age of six, Mozart play the piano beautifully. (He had the musical talent and skill at that young age.)
    ✅ could — playing the piano was his ability/talent in the past.
  • 🏊
    When Meena was five years old, she swim across the pond easily. (She had the physical ability to swim at that age.)
    ✅ could — swimming across the pond was her past ability.
  • 📵
    Irma found she not put down her phone even when she was eating. (She was unable to stop — she lacked the ability to resist.)
    ✅ could not — this shows a negative past ability (she was unable to stop herself).
  • 1
    "She could ride for a long time without getting tired" — does this mean it was her duty, OR that she had the ability/stamina to ride?
  • 2
    Write a sentence using could about something you were able to do when you were very young.
WHAT IT DOES: could shows PAST ABILITY — what someone was able (or unable) to do in the past.
CLUE WORDS: "without getting tired", "able to", "could not", "when he was young", "at the age of"
GRAMMAR: could is always followed by a BASE VERB — could ride, could play, could not take (NOT could rode / could playing).
Phase 3 · Explain The Golden Rule Summary Table
Helper Word What Function Does It Do? Clue Words to Look For Grammar Secret
should Duty or obligation — what is right, important, or good advice. important · right · must · healthy · good habit Always followed by a base verb — should be, should go
would Past habits — things someone did again and again as a routine in the past. every day · every evening · early in the morning · when I was young Always followed by a base verb — would go, would have
could Past ability — what someone was able or unable to do in the past. without getting tired · able to · could not · at the age of Always followed by a base verb — could ride, could not take

📖 Textbook Passage — "What if Irma were living in our times?" (Page 42)

Irma loved spending time with her friends. In the evening she would go cycling. She could ride for a long time without getting tired. Early in the morning she would have great fun drawing and painting. But everything changed when she got a smartphone for her birthday. At first, Irma used her phone for talking to her friends and taking photos. But as time went on, she found she could not take her eyes off the screen. She would scroll through social media, watch videos, and play games for hours. Often she could not help reaching for her phone even when she was busy with other things. Irma's mother noticed this and she thought Irma should not let herself be carried away by the smartphone. Spending time outdoors and enjoying real-life moments should be just as important as the digital world to her.
