The 5-Senses Detective
Every describing word wakes up one of your five senses
Pattern Examples
👁️ How things LOOK
  • beautiful sunset — lovely to see
  • imposing palace — huge & grand
  • colourful festival — full of bright colours
  • crowded market — packed with people
  • breathtaking view — so amazing it takes your breath away
👂 How things SOUND
  • quiet library — almost no sound
  • noisy bus stand — full of loud horns
  • peaceful morning — calm & silent
  • melodious bird song — sweet & musical
👅 How things TASTE
  • sweet payasam — full of sugar
  • salty sea water — tastes like salt
  • spicy pickle — hot with chillies
  • crispy banana chips — crunchy to bite
👃 How things SMELL
  • fragrant jasmine — sweet, pleasant

🤲 How things FEEL
  • cool breeze — gentle cold wind
  • soft white sand — smooth under feet

  • safe park — protected from harm

WHAT THEY DO: Describing words (adjectives) describe people, places, and things.
5 SENSES: They tell us how something LOOKS, SOUNDS, TASTES, SMELLS, or FEELS.

2
Where Does the Describing Word Stand?
Pattern A — before the noun · Pattern B — after is/was/looked/felt
Pattern Examples
Pattern A
We walked through a green forest.  ·  It was a breathtaking waterfall.
Children played in the lively park.  ·  I saw an imposing fort.
→ Describing word comes RIGHT BEFORE the noun (thing/place).
Pattern B
The beach was quiet and serene.  ·  The Burj Khalifah looked imposing.
The forest was green and peaceful.  ·  The park felt lively and safe.
→ Describing word comes AFTER linking words: is, was, were, looked, felt.
Fill in the Blanks
  • 🏖️
    We walked on the , sand. → Describing words BEFORE the noun.
    ✅ soft and white both come right before the noun sand — Pattern A!
  • 🌊
    The sea was and . → Describing words AFTER was.
    ✅ calm and blue come after was — Pattern B!
  • 🎡
    The fair felt and . → Describing words AFTER felt.
    ✅ lively and vibrant come after felt — Pattern B!
  • 1
    In "a peaceful lake", does the describing word come before or after the noun?
  • 2
    In "The lake was peaceful", does the describing word come before or after the linking word was?
PATTERN A: Describing word + Noun → "a peaceful lake" · "the green forest"
PATTERN B: Noun + is/was/looked/felt + Describing word → "The lake was peaceful" · "The forest looked green"
BOTH OK: Both patterns mean the same thing! You can use whichever sounds better in your sentence.

Opposites — Calm Words vs Busy Words
Learning opposite pairs is the fastest way to remember new words
Pattern Examples
Calm / Still Word↔Busy / Loud Word
quiet — almost no sound↔noisy — full of loud sounds
peaceful / serene — calm & still↔busy / bustling — full of fast activity
empty — no people around↔crowded — packed with people
safe — no harm or danger↔dangerous — risky, can cause harm
Fill in the Blanks
  • 🌃
    In the morning the park was , but by evening it was .
    ✅ empty (no one around) → opposite is crowded (full of people).
  • 📖
    The exam hall was . The railway station was .
    ✅ quiet (almost no sound) ↔ noisy (full of loud sounds).
  • ⚠️
    The shallow part of the sea is . The deep part is .
    ✅ safe (no harm) ↔ dangerous (risky, can hurt you).

TIP: Learning words in pairs (quiet ↔ noisy, empty ↔ crowded) helps you remember both words at the same time!
USE THEM: When you write about a place that changes — from morning to evening, or from weekday to festival day — you can use both the calm word AND the busy word in the same paragraph.
4
Word Power Upgrade!
Simple word → Power word from Page 33
Pattern Examples
  • 🔼 "very big and grand" → imposing  (an imposing fort, an imposing waterfall)
  • 🔼 "very calm and quiet" → serene  (a serene backwater lake, a serene morning)
  • 🔼 "very active and full of people" → bustling  (a bustling street market)
  • 🔼 "full of life, music, and colour" → vibrant / lively  (a vibrant festival, a lively fair)
  • 🔼 "so beautiful it takes your breath away" → breathtaking  (a breathtaking mountain view)
Fill in the Blanks
  • 🏔️
    The view from the top of the hill was so beautiful — it was truly !
    ✅ breathtaking = so beautiful that you gasp and cannot breathe for a second!
  • 🏰
    The ancient palace was very big and grand — it looked truly .
    ✅ imposing = very big, tall, and grand — it makes you feel small!
  • 🎪
    The Onam festival street was full of people, music, and colour. It was and .
    ✅ vibrant = full of life and colour. bustling = full of busy activity.

POWER WORDS: very calm = serene · very big/grand = imposing · full of life = vibrant/lively · full of busy people = bustling · so beautiful you gasp = breathtaking
WHY USE THEM? Power words make your writing sound like a proper author! They paint a clearer, more exciting picture in the reader's mind.
Phase 3 · Explain The Describing Word Formula

🎓 The Camera Word Formula

1
WHAT THEY DO: Describing words describe people, places, and things (nouns). They paint pictures!
2
WHAT THEY TELL US: How something looks, sounds, tastes, smells, or feels. (5 Senses!)
3
WAY 1 — Before the noun: "We visited a peaceful village." (describing word + noun)
4
WAY 2 — After is/was/looked/felt: "The village was peaceful." (noun + linking word + describing word)

🏔️ Munnar Hill Station

Last month, I visited the beautiful hill station of Munnar. Away from our busy town, Munnar felt very quiet and peaceful. Everywhere I looked, I saw green tea gardens. In the morning, the white mist gave the valley a serene appearance. The view from the top was truly breathtaking!

🎡 Village Festival Fair

Usually, our village ground is quiet and empty, but that evening it was bustling with people. The ground looked colourful and pretty with thousands of bright lights. It was crowded and noisy with drumbeats. The night fireworks were breathtaking!

🏖️ Cherai Beach

When we reached at four o'clock, the beach was quiet, peaceful, and almost empty. The serene blue sea and green coconut palms looked very beautiful. By five o'clock, the beach became busy, lively, and vibrant. The colourful sunset was truly breathtaking.

💧 Athirappilly Waterfalls

We walked through a green, peaceful forest path. As we got closer, the quiet forest became noisy with the roar of water. The imposing waterfall crashing down the rocks was beautiful and breathtaking. The platform was crowded and bustling with happy tourists.
